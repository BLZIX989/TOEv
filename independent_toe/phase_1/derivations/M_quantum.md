# Node M — Quantum Mechanics

## Independent reconstruction

**Stated (external, Stone's theorem):** a strongly continuous one-parameter group of *unitary*
operators is generated by a self-adjoint operator H via U(t)=exp(−iHt/ħ). This is the mathematical
content underlying "why Schrödinger dynamics has this form," given that quantum evolution must be
reversible/norm-preserving.

**Independent numerical test**, explicitly targeting the Special Rule ("do not identify graph
eigenvalues with physical energy eigenvalues... require an explicit correspondence"): took the toy
Hamiltonian H:=L (the graph Laplacian itself — a real symmetric, hence self-adjoint, operator) on
all 9 PRF-PRIM benchmark graphs. Simulated U(t)=exp(−iLt) numerically at t=1.3:

- **Unitarity error ‖U†U−I‖:** 4.4×10⁻¹⁶ to 1.3×10⁻¹⁵ (machine precision) on all 9 graphs.
- **Norm after unitary evolution:** exactly 1.000000 on all 9 graphs (norm preserved).
- **Norm after the SAME-graph heat semigroup** exp(−Lt) at the same t: shrinks to 0.11–0.59
  depending on the graph — a genuine **contraction**, matching PRF-PRIM's earlier finding that
  exp(−tL) is not volume-preserving.

This makes the distinction concrete on the identical operator family: the SAME L generates a
genuine unitary group (i inserted) and a genuine contraction semigroup (no i) — visibly different
physical behavior from the same spectral data.

Output: `../M_quantum/quantum_unitarity_results.json`. Script: `M_quantum.py`.

## The correspondence λ_n^graph ↔ E_n^physical

**Explicitly NOT derived.** The identification "H:=L, hence E_n:=ħλ_n" used above is a **choice**
made by this repository's own toy construction, not something derived from graph structure. No
argument establishing this correspondence independently was found anywhere in the corpus or
constructed here. Per the Special Rule, this is preserved as **CANDIDATE**, not upgraded to
CALCULATED or DERIVED.
