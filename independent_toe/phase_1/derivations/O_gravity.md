ds²=−dt²+a(t)²(dx²+dy²+dz²), computed the Christoffel symbols directly from the metric-compatibility
formula, then the Riemann tensor from its definition (in terms of Christoffel derivatives and
products), then the Ricci tensor (contraction), then the Ricci scalar R (full contraction). Result:
**R = 6(ä/a + (ȧ/a)²) exactly**, matching the standard textbook formula with **zero symbolic
discrepancy** — independently computed, not looked up.
